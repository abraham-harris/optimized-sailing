# Optimized Water Travel

This repo contains work in optimal control to navigate watercraft from one point to another under a variety of conditions:
- Motorboat subject to strong currents
- Sailboat in the wind (must tack to move into a headwind)
- wind and water currents with a sail

In all of these scenarios we try to minimize time to destination, fuel usage (when applicable and under a variety of fuel costs), and (sometimes) speed at arrival.
